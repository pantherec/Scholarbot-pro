## Second Post Idea — The Numbers Hook

**Post text:**

62% of scholarship providers now use AI detection tools.

That means students who use ChatGPT or generic AI writers to draft their scholarship essays are getting flagged — and possibly disqualified.

But here's the thing: the problem isn't AI. The problem is lazy AI.

Generic outputs that start with "I am writing to express my interest..." or lean on words like "delve" and "cutting-edge" — those get caught. Because they sound like every other AI-generated essay.

A student's real story — their specific experiences, their grandmother's name, the date they won that science fair, the part-time job at the hardware store — that's what sounds human. Because it is.

At MeritLaunch, we built our letter generation around this principle. Four writing style templates, anti-detection rules baked in, and biographical details woven throughout. The AI handles structure and time savings. The student's voice stays their own.

Your story is the application. We just help you tell it.
