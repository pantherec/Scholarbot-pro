Dear Scholarship Committee,

The corn was going to be lost. It was nine at night in October of my sophomore year, rain was coming, and my neighbor Dale Petersen's auger had jammed. I had spent a year building robots in Mr. Loger's shop, so I could see what the problem was: a sheared bolt on the drive. I fixed it with the shop light in my teeth while Dale held the flashlight. The corn came in ahead of the rain.

What stayed with me was not the repair. Anybody around Hartley can fix an auger. It was understanding why it sheared, and realizing that machines get designed for ideal operators while farms have tired ones. That is why I want to study agricultural engineering at Iowa State.

I have been building toward this for a while. I captain our FTC robotics team, and as vice president of our FFA chapter I ran the fruit sale that raised $11,400, a chapter record. I rebuilt a 1968 Feterl grain auger for $212 in parts and kept every receipt. I work about 15 hours a week at Van's Feed & Seed, and on weeknights I watch my brother Tyler until Mom gets home from her second job. I am second in my class of 91 with a 3.98 GPA.

Nobody in my family has a four-year degree, and I will be the first to apply. I want to come back to northwest Iowa and build affordable precision equipment for the small farms nobody designs for. Thank you for reading my application for the Heartland Rural Futures Scholarship.

Sincerely,
Jenna Ostercamp
