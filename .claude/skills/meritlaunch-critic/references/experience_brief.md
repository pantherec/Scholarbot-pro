# Key Experience Moment brief

A Key Experience Moment is the point where a student decides "this gets me", "I can do this",
"this sounds like me" or "I'm coming back", or a parent decides "this is legitimate" and "this is
worth paying for". Each brief must be buildable in one lane and must pass the product rules in
SKILL.md §3.

Use this format for every brief:

```
### KEM-<n>: <short name>
- **Who:** student or parent.
- **When:** the exact trigger (route, step, condition).
- **Today:** what they see and feel now, with evidence (screenshot, file:line, measure).
- **The moment:** what they should see and feel instead, in one or two sentences.
- **Build:** components, copy and data; files touched; new tests.
- **Rules check:** why it keeps the student the author, invents nothing, shares no student data,
  states only true numbers, and keeps the under-13 gate.
- **Measure of success:** the number or observation that proves it landed.
- **Sets up:** the later moment it makes possible, if any.
```
